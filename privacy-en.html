Life Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience. The Japanese version (プライバシーポリシー) is the legally binding text; if the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

"Life" (the "App") is developed and provided by Taro Eiyo (known as "Neil") as an individual developer (the "Developer"). This Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Our Principle: Your Data Stays on Your Device

The App is a lifelogging app whose purpose is to turn everyday life into memories without effort. By its nature it handles private information such as photos and location, but all analysis and diary generation happen only on your device. Photos, videos, diary entries, location, and Health data are never sent to the Developer's servers, and the Developer cannot see them. Only when you use the partner connection feature, the information needed for sync (plans, anniversaries, display name (nickname), Premium status, and an anonymous identifier) is stored on the sync server. The Developer can technically access the information on the sync server but uses it only to keep sync working and to investigate problems.

Limits on Sharing and Use

  • Except for the external services above (processors), the Developer does not provide your information to third parties, unless disclosure is required by law.
  • Health data, location, and photos are never used for advertising, marketing, or sale. The App shows no advertising.

Disclosures under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (Japan)

  • Name and address of the business operator handling personal information: Taro Eiyo (the address will be provided without delay on request)
  • Purposes of use: As stated in the table "Information We Collect and How We Use It". The information is not used for any other purpose.
  • Processors and third parties in foreign countries: Sync processing for the partner connection feature is entrusted to Supabase, Inc. (US company; data stored in Japan, AWS Tokyo region), and subscription verification to RevenueCat, Inc. (US company; data stored in the US). Both are "third parties in a foreign country (the United States)" under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information. The United States has no comprehensive personal information protection law covering businesses in general like Japan's; instead it has sector-specific federal and state laws (see the Personal Information Protection Commission's survey of foreign data protection systems). Both companies handle data only within the scope of the Developer's instructions under data processing agreements, and apply safeguards such as encryption of data in transit and at rest, access controls, and third-party security audits. By turning on the partner connection feature (issuing or entering a secret code) and by making an in-app purchase, you consent to the provision (entrustment) to the foreign third parties above.
  • Summary of security measures: Encryption in transit (TLS); row-level access control on sync data (readable and writable only by you and your registered partner); anonymous IDs with no email address or phone number collected; two-factor authentication on the Developer's admin accounts; awareness of the external environment when handling personal data abroad, as described above.
  • Disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, and suspension of use of retained personal data: You can delete data on the sync server yourself in the App via "Remove partner" or "Settings → Privacy → Delete All Data". Requests are also accepted at the contact address below (we may ask you to confirm your registration details in the App for identity verification; no fee is charged).
  • Sensitive personal information: Information that may qualify as sensitive personal information, such as Health data, is processed only on the device and is not sent to the Developer or any third party.
  • Complaints: lifeapp.support@gmail.com
  • Users under 16: Consent to the collection of information from users under 16, and to its provision to third parties in foreign countries, must be given by a parent or legal guardian.

Family Check-in Feature (Planned for a Future Update)

Not currently provided. When provided, it will follow these principles, and this Policy will be revised accordingly.

  1. Parental consent: Starting check-in requires explicit action on both the parent's and the child's device (linking by code and approval).
  2. No hidden monitoring of children: The child's device will always show that check-in is active.
  3. Automatic limits as the child grows: The information shared narrows automatically by school year, and sharing of current location and photos ends automatically when the child enters high school.
  4. Children under 13 may use the feature only under parental management.

Deleting and Exporting Data

  • All users, including those on the free plan, can export and delete their data at any time in the App via "Settings → Privacy".
  • Permissions for Photos, Location, Health, Motion, Calendar, and so on can be revoked at any time in iOS "Settings → Privacy & Security". Collection of that information stops as soon as the permission is revoked.
  • Data synced through the partner connection feature is also deleted from the sync server when you remove your partner or choose "Delete All Data". If you delete the App without doing either, the data on the sync server remains until your partner removes the connection (the Developer will delete it on request).
  • There is currently no backup feature (planned for a future update). Deleting the App deletes the data on your device, so export your data first if you need it.
  • Cancelling a paid plan never deletes your data (some features are simply locked).
  • Retention periods: Data on the device, until you delete it; Life Log diary records, the most recent 60 days; data on the sync server, until the partner connection is removed; purchase information, for as long as needed to manage the subscription.

Changes to This Policy
